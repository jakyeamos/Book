# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers use Book alone, often on a phone or laptop, to move through a chapter and its atmosphere without wrestling with interface chrome. Editors use Author Studio to shape text, visual mood, soundtrack, and ambience as one publishable work.

## Product Purpose

Book is a narrative reading and composition platform. Its distinctive value is the relationship between prose and a carefully authored soundscape: music and ambience should make a chapter feel intentional without becoming a distraction. A successful release lets an editor compose that relationship precisely and lets a reader experience it calmly, accessibly, and reliably.

## Brand Personality

Immersive, animated, classy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon/cyberpunk control rooms, decorative skeuomorphic book UI, noisy always-on motion, and interfaces that make the authoring tool feel more important than the work.

## Design Principles

- The work is the interface: prose and atmosphere lead; controls recede until needed.
- Sound is composition, not an attachment: scenes, layers, transitions, and intent remain visible to authors.
- Calm confidence: every state is legible, reversible, and free of mystery during editing and publishing.
- Atmosphere has boundaries: chapter mood can change the surface without reducing contrast, accessibility, or navigation clarity.
- One truth: reader rendering and Studio preview use the same versioned document and experience model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide semantic landmarks, keyboard-complete workflows, visible focus, labelled controls, 44px touch targets where practical, live async status, focus-restoring dialogs, strong contrast, and reduced-motion behavior. Audio never starts without explicit user consent and remains independently controllable.
